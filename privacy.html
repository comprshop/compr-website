Privacy Policy

How COMPR handles your data

Updated August 25, 2026 for the free public beta.

What COMPR processes

The extension reads publicly visible product-page information needed to detect, save, compare or search for a product. This can include product name, brand, price, image, URL, store, model and product identifiers, category, colour, size, storage, material, availability and other variant details.

When you create an account, COMPR processes your email address, user ID, authentication session, plan, search usage and subscription status. COMPR does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

Saved products

Saved products and extension preferences are stored locally in your browser using browser-extension storage. They can remain on that browser after you sign out. You can remove them using Clear all or by uninstalling the extension. They are not automatically synchronized between devices.

Find This Cheaper

COMPR only sends relevant product details to its backend when you start a price check. The backend authenticates the request, applies usage controls and may send a product search query to the configured search provider. Candidate retailer pages are validated before results are shown. Search requests, outcomes and limited operational information may be retained to enforce quotas, prevent abuse, diagnose failures and improve reliability.

Early-access interest

If you join early access, COMPR stores your email address, selected future plan and signup source with your free account. This records interest only and does not create a purchase or subscription.

Services used

  • Supabase for authentication, account, early-access interest, entitlement and usage data.
  • Brave Search API for web-search candidates when Find This Cheaper or deal search is used.
  • European Central Bank reference rates for indicative currency conversion.
  • GitHub Pages for this public website.

These providers may process data under their own terms and privacy notices.

Security and retention

COMPR limits account mutations to server-side services. Account and early-access data is kept while the beta account remains active or until a valid deletion request is completed. Short-lived operational and security records may be retained longer when needed to prevent abuse, diagnose failures or comply with legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

You can clear locally saved products in the extension and request access, correction or deletion of account and early-access data through support. You may also withdraw early-access interest at any time by emailing support.
