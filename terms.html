The service

COMPR helps users save and compare product information and search for matching retailer offers. Prices, availability, shipping, product identity and retailer information can change or be incomplete. COMPR does not guarantee the cheapest price, continuous availability or that every candidate is an exact match. Verify the retailer page before buying.

Free beta

COMPR is currently offered as a free public beta. Features, limits and availability may change as the service is tested and improved. No paid subscription is offered through this public beta.

Accounts and acceptable use

You must provide accurate account information and keep sign-in links and sessions secure. You may not automate account creation, evade quotas or rate limits, interfere with the service, scrape it, resell access or use it unlawfully.

Search usage

A successful search is counted when COMPR accepts and returns at least one useful live match under the applicable matching rules. Errors, timeouts, duplicate requests, demo data and searches without an accepted useful match are not intended to consume usage.

Early access

Joining Pro or Ultimate early access records non-binding interest only. It is not an order, does not reserve a final price and does not authorize COMPR to take payment. Explicit agreement will be required before any future paid service starts.

Availability and responsibility

The beta may contain errors or be interrupted. COMPR is a comparison aid and does not sell the retailer products shown. You remain responsible for checking the retailer, product, price, delivery terms and availability before buying.
