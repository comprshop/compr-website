Support

How can we help?

Support for the free public beta.

Search result issue

Include the retailer URL, the product you expected and a screenshot. Never send passwords, sign-in links, payment-card details or private API keys.

Founding Access

Joining the Pro or Ultimate interest list is free and non-binding. It does not create an order, collect payment details or reserve one of the first 1,000 discounted activations.

Clear saved products

Use Clear all on the comparison page. Signing out does not automatically remove products stored locally in that browser.

Account deletion

Email support from the address connected to your account. COMPR will verify the request before deleting eligible account data.
